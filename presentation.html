NexGen QA Live Detection

Smart Circuit Board Inspection

A live camera system to spot manufacturing errors instantly.



Presented by: Saad Ullah

The Problem

Checking electronics by hand on a fast factory line is very difficult:

  • Human Eyes Get Tired: It is easy for a person to miss a tiny scratch or a broken wire after hours of working.
  • Speed: Factory lines move too fast for manual checking.
  • Old Software: Traditional computer programs often freeze or lag when trying to process live video.

The Solution

We built a smart camera system to check Printed Circuit Boards (PCBs) automatically.

  • We showed the computer examples of 6 common mistakes (like a missing hole or a broken connection).
  • The system studied these images and learned exactly what the mistakes look like.
  • Now, it can scan a live video and spot those exact problems in milliseconds.

How It Works

When a circuit board is placed under the camera:

  1. The live video is sent to our fast cloud server.
  2. The system scans every single frame of the video as it happens.
  3. If it sees a mistake, it draws a colored box right on top of it on the screen and tells us what the error is.

The Software Behind It

To make the video run smoothly without freezing, the software is split into two parts:

  • The Dashboard (Website): A clean, easy-to-use screen that turns on the camera and shows the live results.
  • The Brain (Server): A fast backend that does all the heavy math.
  • They talk to each other instantly using a continuous live connection, ensuring there is zero lag.

Looking Forward

This system is built to be flexible. Right now it checks circuit boards, but we can easily teach it to check other things just by showing it new pictures:

  • Cracks in Solar Panels.
  • Scratches on Car Parts.
  • Bad seals on Food Packaging.
